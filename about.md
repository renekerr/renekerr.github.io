## Who am I

I'm Rene, a Data Engineer and DBA based in Spain, exploring and diving into cybersecurity.

I've been working with databases for years — SQL, query optimization, data pipelines.

That background turned out to be a solid foundation for understanding how systems can be abused from the inside.

About a year ago I started learning ethical hacking and I haven't stopped since.

## What is this blog

Low Priv Notes is where I document what I learn — but with a specific focus.

Most CTF writeups show the fastest or easiest path to the flag.

I'm more interested in what happens when you take a different road.

This blog explores alternative exploitation and privilege escalation vectors — the ones most people skip because they already have root.

No flags. No shortcuts. Just methodology.
